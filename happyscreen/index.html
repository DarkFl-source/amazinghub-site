HappyScreen

HappyScreen turns your Android TV into a slideshow of the photos and videos you pick in Google Photos, set to a soundtrack made from songs you send from your phone. Made for get-togethers with friends and family: everything works with just the TV remote, and your photos and soundtrack stay only on your TV.
