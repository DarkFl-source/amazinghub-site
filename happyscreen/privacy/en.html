HappyScreen Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

HappyScreen is an Android TV app that shows, as a slideshow, photos that you pick in Google Photos, with music that you send from your phone. HappyScreen has no servers: everything the app stores stays on your own TV.

1. Data HappyScreen accesses

  • Photos you select in Google Photos. HappyScreen uses the Google Photos Picker API with the photospicker.mediaitems.readonly permission, which grants access only to the photos you pick in each selection. The app cannot see the rest of your library.
  • Videos you select, when “Include videos” is on: downloaded to the TV like the photos, with one frame of the video used as a thumbnail.
  • Selected photo data: the image file (resized for the TV screen), its date and dimensions, used to display and order the show.
  • Google access credential: a refresh token stored encrypted on the TV with a system key (Android Keystore), so you do not have to connect again every time.
  • Songs you send from your phone over the local Wi-Fi network, stored on the TV to play during shows.

2. How data is used

Only to create and display your photo shows on your TV. HappyScreen does not send photos, music or credentials to any HappyScreen or third-party server, does not show ads, does not run analytics and does not sell or share data.

3. Where data is kept

  • Photos, music and settings: in the app's internal storage on your TV.
  • During sign-in, your phone passes through a static HappyScreen page (amazinghub.app/happyscreen/oauth/callback.html) that only forwards Google's response to your TV on the local network. That page does not store or send any information anywhere else, and the authorization code that passes through it only works together with a secret that never leaves the TV (PKCE).
  • Sending music happens directly between your phone and your TV on the local network, protected by a one-time code, and only works while the sending screen is open.

4. Deleting your data

  • Deleting a photo show removes its photos from the TV.
  • Deleting a song in the Library removes the file from the TV.
  • Settings → Disconnect account revokes HappyScreen's access at Google and deletes the credential from the TV. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its data from the TV.

5. Use of Google data

HappyScreen's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Children

HappyScreen is not directed to children and does not collect data to identify any user.

7. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. Questions: contato@amazinghub.app.
